Sunlight becomes stored backup power

One simple energy flow, from daylight to a connected phone, in four steps.

Collect

Solar panel collects energy during the day.

Manage

Charge controller manages the flow safely.

Store

Battery stores energy for shade, evening, or cloudy periods.

Deliver

Fast-charge ports deliver power when a user connects a phone.

Four things the unit has to survive

Heat

Use a shaded, ventilated enclosure and heat-tolerant components.

Dust

Seal key electronics and protect ports with covers.

Portability

Keep the unit light enough to move, but stable enough to stay put.

Security

Add a lock point, tamper-resistant casing, and a simple check-in routine.
